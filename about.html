Who we are

A studio, not a shop.

Vitrine is an independent design studio in the United Kingdom. We build shop windows on the web: pages where a house's pieces are shown the way its own boutiques show them — in its own colours, its own lettering and its own light.

Nothing is sold here. Every button opens the house's official boutique.

  • One voice per house

    Tiffany blue, Cartier red, Chanel black and white — each house keeps its own identity.

  • Film and 3D, not banners

    Seamless looping films and real-time 3D pieces you can turn and open.

  • Royalty-free imagery

    All photography and film is licensed stock. No brand campaign material is used.

What we do

Windows for great houses

We design and build the whole window — from the first frame of film to the last button — and test it on phones, tablets and large monitors.

Start a project

  • House pages

    A page per house with its signatures, its story and a direct door to its boutique.

  • Looping films

    Short films cut to loop without a seam, for full screen, phones and in-store displays.

  • 3D objects

    Diamonds and bottles that turn, sparkle and open a film when touched.

  • Every screen

    Built mobile-first, checked edge to edge on wide monitors.
