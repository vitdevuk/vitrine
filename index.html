About Vitrine

A shop window, not a shop.

Vitrine is an independent showcase. It gathers the houses people search for most in fine jewellery and beauty, presents each one in its own colours and type, and sends you straight to the brand's own boutique to buy.

More about the studio →

  • Official doors only

    Every “Visit” button opens the house's own website. Nothing is resold here.

  • Each house, its own voice

    Colours and lettering on every card follow the brand's identity — Tiffany blue, Cartier red, Chanel black and white.

  • Royalty-free imagery

    All photography is licensed stock. No brand campaign images are used.
